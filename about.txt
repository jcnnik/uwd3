Universal Watermark Disabler 3 ("UWD3") v{}
Based on UWD2 by Melody https://machineonamission.me/
Source: https://github.com/machineonamission/uwd2
Written in Rust

Improvements over UWD2:
  - Multi-pattern binary scan (resilient to compiler prologue changes)
  - Structural GDI-call scan (finds the watermark function with zero cached state)
  - install-task subcommand for automatic re-patching on logon
